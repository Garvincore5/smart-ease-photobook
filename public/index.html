Smart Ease Tutorials & Quick Guide
📖 1. Designing Spreads & Drag & Drop
Drag photos directly from the bottom photo tray onto the spread. Smart Ease will automatically choose the best matching layout from 200 realistic templates based on photo orientation!
📐 2. Resizing Boxes Independently (Aspect Ratio Locked)
Touch or click any frame on the spread to select it. Drag any of the 4 circular corner handles to resize that box independently—the aspect ratio is strictly preserved with zero distortion!
🎨 3. Right-Side Photo Inspector (Adjustments & Mirror)
Clicking any photo opens the Right Inspector with high-res large preview, Flip Horizontally (Mirror), Flip Vertically, Crop/Framing (Fill vs Fit), Light & Color sliders (Brightness, Contrast, Warmth), and instant Black & White (Saturation 0%).
